[The VC Corner](https://www.thevccorner.com/p/anthropic-s1-ipo-filing), [Guru3D](https://www.guru3d.com/story/anthropic-ipo-filing-shows-billion-first-half-accounting-loss/), [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/three-takeaways-from-anthropics-s-1)
