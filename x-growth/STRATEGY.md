# @aleromlan growth plan

## Positioning
A 24-year-old builder who explains what is actually happening in AI and what it means for founders and normal people. Calm, specific, a little contrarian. Not a hype account.

Content pillars:
1. **AI in the real world (~40%)**: medicine, science, hard numbers. Take a big headline, explain what's actually interesting about it, and say the caveat out loud. The caveats are what make people trust the account.
2. **Startup culture (~35%)**: small teams, fundraising, S-1s, how company building is changing.
3. **Being young in this moment (~15%)**: the honest, personal angle. This works best with real details from you.
4. **Questions (~10%)**: open questions that get replies, since replies are one of the strongest reach signals.

## Formats
- One single post a day, plus one long post (500–1,500 chars) one or two times a week. No threads.
- Long posts need X Premium. Premium also gets a reach boost, so it's worth the money here.
- Articles for occasional deep dives (for example, "AI in medicine: what's real in Q4 2026").
- Never put links in the main post. Put the source in the first reply.
- Add an image or chart when there is one (native media gets more reach than plain text).

## Cadence (UTC, US-friendly)
- ~14:40: main post (US morning)
- ~20:40: second post on Tue/Thu (long post or question)
- Weekly: look at what performed and adjust the mix.

## Voice rules
- Write like a person talking: short sentences, no buzzwords, no "game-changer", no em dashes, no emoji walls, no hashtags.
- Every factual claim gets checked against at least two sources before it's posted.
- No made-up personal stories. Personal posts only use details Alex has given.

## What stays manual (X automation rules and spam risk)
Automated replies, likes, follows and DMs get accounts flagged. Those stay with Alex, and they are the biggest growth lever for a small account:
- **15 min/day replying thoughtfully to 5–10 bigger AI/startup accounts.** Early, substantive replies on big accounts are how small accounts get discovered.
- Reply to everyone who comments in the first hour after a post.

## Profile
- Bio idea: "24. Building in AI. Writing about what's actually happening in AI, medicine and startups."
- Pin the best-performing long post after week 1.
