Uncomfortable fact about AI drug discovery: there still isn't a single approved drug that was discovered mainly by AI.

The data underneath is more interesting than either the hype or the cynicism.

AI-designed molecules pass Phase 1 trials 80 to 90% of the time. That's far above the historical average. Phase 1 mostly asks "is this safe?"

Phase 2 asks "does it actually work?" There, AI-discovered drugs succeed about 40% of the time. Roughly the same as everyone else.

So AI got very good at designing molecules the body tolerates. It hasn't yet gotten better at picking the right biology to go after.

That second problem is the big one. And I doubt it gets solved by a better chemistry model alone.
