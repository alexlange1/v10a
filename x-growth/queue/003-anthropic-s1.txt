Anthropic's S-1 is the most interesting startup document of the year.

Revenue:
2023: $41M
2024: $386M
2025: $4.6B
First half of 2026 alone: $16.3B

The scary-looking $80.7B net loss? Around $72B of it is an accounting effect from their own valuation going up. Q2 was their first operating profit, roughly $974M.

Two things I'd take from it as a founder:

1. "AI companies can't make money" aged badly. Fast.
2. About 20% of revenue comes from two customers. Even at this scale, concentration risk doesn't go away.

Every company is a private startup until the S-1. Then everyone gets to read the diary.
