AI took 64% of all venture money in the world last quarter. $102B out of $159B.

And that was the "slow" quarter. It was closer to 80% in Q1, mostly because OpenAI and Anthropic didn't raise this time.

27 companies raised billion-dollar rounds and took about a third of everything.

If you're raising for a non-AI company right now, you're not competing with other startups for attention. You're competing with a handful of giants for the same money.

The upside: almost nobody is looking at what you're doing. That's usually where the good contrarian bets are.
