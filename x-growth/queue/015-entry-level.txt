The people AI is hitting first aren't senior workers. They're people my age.

A Stanford study found employment for 22 to 25 year olds in the most AI-exposed jobs fell about 13% relative to everyone else. Older workers in the same jobs were fine.

The entry-level job was where you learned by doing the boring stuff. If AI does the boring stuff, how does the next generation learn?

Nobody has a good answer yet. The companies that figure out how to train juniors in this world will have the best people in 10 years.
