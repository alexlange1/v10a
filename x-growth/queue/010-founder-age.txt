The average YC founder is now about 26, according to one YC investor's analysis. It was about 29 a few years ago.

The average age of founders behind the fastest-growing US startups, in a well-known MIT and Census study? 45.

Both can be true. Young founders take more shots. Older founders land a higher share of them.

At 24 my takeaway isn't "wait." It's take shots like you're young, and learn like you're 45.
